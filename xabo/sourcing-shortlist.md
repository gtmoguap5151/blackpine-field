## ⚠️ The finding that changes the plan

**Nobody stocks Matt's mechanism.** Across Alibaba, Made-in-China, and Accio, there is no off-the-shelf keychain body with a flip-out arm holding one reversible flat+Phillips bit that locks. The stock market offers three different animals instead:

1. **Bit-holder keychains** — bits stored on a ring/carabiner, plugged into a separate socket. No flip-out arm, no lock.
2. **Pen-style reversible pocket drivers** — the bit pulls out, flips, and reinserts. Reversible, yes; flip-out locking keychain body, no.
3. **Generic folding keychain multitools** — fixed drivers ground into the body, and **nearly every one includes a bottle opener** (cut from the spec) in unstated or bottom-grade steel.

So Lane 1 cannot produce the XABO as designed. Its real value: **cheap torture-test benchmarks and components** (especially proper S2 reversible bits) to learn what breaks before tooling money is spent. **The actual XABO is a Lane 2 product** — or a hybrid: a Lane 2 factory modifying an existing folding-body platform (ODM) rather than full custom tooling, which cuts tooling cost and time.

**Lane 2 red flags / spec notes**
- **420 / 3Cr13 is the floor, not abuse-grade.** For the flip-bit arm and lock, spec **440A (7Cr17MoV) minimum, D2 or 14C28N preferred**; bits in **S2 tool steel, HRC 58+**. Kegani is the only shortlisted factory publishing steels above 440-class with mill certificates.
- Tooling ($500–$5,000+) is the real Lane 2 gate, not unit price. An **ODM variant** (factory's existing folding platform, modified: opener deleted, bit arm + lock added, black finish, amber ring) can dodge most tooling — ask Kegani and Plenty Harvest to quote both full-OEM and modified-platform.
- Matte black on steel = black oxide / DLC-style coating; on aluminum = anodize. Confirm coating wear expectations — the torture test will strip cheap coatings on camera, which is fine for content but bad if it reads as product failure.
- US sample freight (both lanes): not published on listings; budget **$8–25 per supplier** for small express parcels. Estimates, not quotes.
