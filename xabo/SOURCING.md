# XABO — Sourcing Shortlist (prep, checked 2026-10-23)

Matt picks the lane ("one word"); this sheet makes the pick an order. All figures
are marketplace snapshots from 2026-10-23 web research (Accio/B2B indexes + factory
guides) — NOT live checkout prices. The payment rule holds: exact final total is
verified on the order page and stated to Matt in chat before anything moves.
His cards are locked by default; he unlocks the one he wants charged.

## Lane A — stock flip-bit keychain tool body, XABO etched on it
Goal: 4 torture-test samples, etched where possible, ~$20–200 all-in, weeks not months.

1. **Foshan Grace Metal Co., Ltd.** (manufacturer, 8 yrs, 4.9/5.0)
   - Stainless steel keychain multitool, custom logo; listed price $0.30–0.99/pc,
     min order **2 pieces** — samples are effectively the order.
   - Why: lowest-friction sampling; 2896 sold on this listing family, 17% reorder rate.
2. **Yangjiang Yangdong Jiayang Hardware Products Factory** (18 yrs, 5.0/5.0)
   - EDC stainless keychain tool, DIY/OEM, min order **2 pieces**, $0.90.
   - Why: Yangjiang = the tool-manufacturing hub; longest operating history here.
3. **Jiangsu Smiletools Co., Ltd.** (manufacturer, 13 yrs, 4.8/5.0, 17% reorder)
   - 6-in-1 folding keychain multitool with screwdriver bit, $0.98–1.10,
     min order 500 — bulk lane, not samples.
   - Why: the right shape if Lane A graduates to a production run off the stock body.
- Lane A reality check: stock tools are typically 2Cr13/3Cr13 stainless — fine for
  Matt's torture test as *data*, but these will likely break (by design of the
  test). Engraving/etching quotes come with the sample order; expect $15–50/sample
  typical across these suppliers.

## Lane B — custom-tooled to XABO spec
Spec: matte black steel, amber ring, XABO etched; NO opener; reversible flat+Phillips
bit that flips out and locks; lock must survive pry-level torque.

1. **Kegani** (Yangjiang, OEM-first — no competing house brand)
   - Stock/private-label multi-tools from ~100–200 units; custom OEM MOQ 100–500.
   - Steels 8Cr13MoV → D2 → 14C28N → VG10 → M390 with mill certificates —
     pick a real steel grade for the torture standard (recommend 14C28N or D2 min).
   - Prototyping 2–4 weeks; mass production 45–75 days after sample approval;
     first OEM order ~18–22 weeks door-to-door. Written confidentiality,
     dedicated project manager, pre-production sample as binding quality standard.
   - Why: the only shop here that publishes steel grades + a confidentiality line —
     matters if the flip-bit lock geometry is XABO's IP.
2. **VESPER Knives** (Yangjiang)
   - Multi-tools: **1,000–3,000 pcs** MOQ; conventional knives 600; titanium 100.
   - Samples refundable against first $3,000+ bulk order; 30–45 days production
     after deposit + sample approval.
   - Why: the volume/production lane if Lane B works and Matt wants inventory;
     limited third-party history — request business registration + live factory
     walkthrough before any deposit.
3. **LeeKnives** (Yangjiang, since 1993, broadest catalog)
   - Published MOQ 50 pcs/model (third-party listing); 30–45 days mass production
     after sample approval. OEM/ODM + sourcing services.
   - Why: lowest custom MOQ found — the bridge if Lane B but Matt balks at 1,000+.
- Lane B reality check: custom tooling means real tooling/mold cost on top of
  samples ($20–50/sample per B2B norms). 18–22 weeks door-to-door for a first
  custom order is the honest calendar.

## Decision math for Matt (one line each)
- Lane A: spend ~$20–200, have etched samples in weeks, break them on camera,
  learn what the flip-bit lock needs — then decide whether Lane B earns its tooling.
- Lane B: bigger check, real steel, the amber ring and lock geometry are his —
  production funded by Blackpine pre-orders, factory paid from customer cash.
