# XABO — Product Spec (living document, Matt's design calls)

**Product:** XABO everyday-carry tool, sold through Blackpine Field. Working direction: Concept A — keychain multitool (matte black steel, amber accent ring, XABO etched).

## Matt's design decisions
- 2026-10-23: **No bottle opener.** "Completely useless." Cut it entirely.
- 2026-10-23: **Driver = reversible flat + Phillips bit.** One bit, flathead on one end, Phillips on the other — flip it and lock it in place.

## Testing standard (Matt, 2026-10-23)
- Matt: "I promise I will break every single sample sent — I'm going to use the knife like a truck." Sourcing must spec for abuse, not for cheapest unit cost: real steel grade and thickness, a flip-bit lock that survives pry-level torque. Samples that break are data; the survivor earns the XABO etching. Film the torture tests — that footage IS the brand proof and the launch content.

## Name clearance (checked 2026-10-23, per Matt's "check the name's clear" request)
- Domains: **xabo.com is taken** — premium listing on BrandBucket at **$47,995** (or $4,400/mo lease-to-own), registered since 2000, expires 2034. Not worth it for a pre-revenue product. **xeabo.com is also taken** (registered 2006, expires 2027-07-06). **xabogear.com appears available** (no registry record). Working plan: launch the product under Blackpine Field (blackpinefield.com), no fresh domain needed now.
- Trademark: no exact "XABO" federal mark surfaced in the checks run (USPTO-mirrored indexes via TrademarkElite, wordmark search). Closest cousin: **KABO TOOLS** (Reg., Sep 30 2025, Taiwan tool company, tools class) — phonetically adjacent, different spelling. This is a preliminary check, not legal advice; a full attorney clearance is wise before real spend (filing runs ~$350/class govt fee).
