# BLACKPINE FIELD — Brand Guide

**Name:** BLACKPINE FIELD
**Domain:** blackpinefield.com (confirmed available 2026-10-22; purchase pending)

## Who it's for

Outdoors, hiking, camping, survival, and military audiences who buy gear the way
they'd inspect it before a deployment: on the numbers. They value reliability
and durability over novelty, and they can smell hype from a mile off.

## Voice

- **Plain.** Short sentences. No marketing adjectives doing the work that specs
  should do. If a sentence would embarrass us read aloud at a trailhead, cut it.
- **Numbers first.** Lead with the figure that matters under load: fabric denier
  and weave, battery capacity (Wh) and continuous output (W), waterproof rating
  (mm), filter pore size (microns) and rated life (gallons/liters), weight
  limits. Then say what the figure means in the field.
- **No hype.** No "game-changer," no "ultimate," no "military-grade" unless a
  military spec is actually cited. No urgency theater, no fake scarcity.
- **Verdicts, not vibes.** Every piece ends with a clear call: which one, for
  whom, and why — by use case.
- **Honest limits.** State what a product can't do as plainly as what it can.
  Where figures vary by model or colorway, give the range and say so.

## The three operational rules

1. **Emphasize "under load" specs.** Don't say a pack is strong — say it's
   1000D CORDURA® with a 2x2 basket weave, and what that costs in ounces.
   Numbers that matter in the mud: denier, weave, Wh, continuous W, dBA,
   waterproof mm, microns, rated filter life, carry weight.
2. **Visual proof.** Video shows gear being packed, carried, poured, plugged
   in, and stressed — close-ups of seams, zippers, ports, and filter housings.
   Written pieces carry crisp close-up photography of the same details. If we
   don't have the gear in hand, we say the piece is a spec comparison and the
   visuals are manufacturer/B-roll, labeled as such.
3. **Diversify beyond Amazon.** Premium affiliate networks (AvantLink and
   brand-direct programs) are the priority: better rates, longer cookie
   windows, and brands this audience actually trusts (5.11 Tactical, Mystery
   Ranch, GORUCK, EcoFlow, Jackery, Goal Zero, Sawyer, Katadyn). Amazon links
   are the fallback, never the strategy.

## The hard rule — no fake testing

**BLACKPINE FIELD never claims hands-on testing that did not happen.**

- Research and spec-comparison pieces are labeled as exactly that, in the
  video and in the article.
- We never invent test results, runtimes, flow rates, or field anecdotes.
- The day a piece of gear is actually in hand and used, the piece may say so —
  with dates, conditions, and load stated. Until then: "We compared the
  published specs" is the honest sentence, and we print it.

## FTC affiliate disclosure — standard template

Use verbatim at the **top** of every article, and spoken + on-screen in every
video (see each launch-slate script for placement).

> **Disclosure:** BLACKPINE FIELD is reader-supported. Some links on this page
> are affiliate links — if you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no
> extra cost to you. Commissions never change our verdicts. This piece is a
> research and specification comparison; unless it says otherwise in the text,
> we have not hands-on tested the products covered.

Short form (video description / social caption):

> Affiliate disclosure: some links earn us a commission at no cost to you.
> Specs are manufacturer-published figures; this is a research comparison,
> not a hands-on test.

## Format

- **Video first:** 60–90 second vertical shorts (YouTube Shorts / Reels /
  TikTok). Hook in the first 3 seconds, specs on screen, verdict at the end.
- **Written companion:** every video gets an ~800–1,200 word article as the
  link hub — the full comparison table, verdict by use case, and the
  affiliate links live there.
- Publishing anything requires Matt's explicit approval of the exact visual
  and caption first. No exceptions.
